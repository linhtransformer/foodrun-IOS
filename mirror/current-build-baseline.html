Observations for the redesign. The showcase screen is a gallery, not a worker home — there is no navigation shell at all, so every screen lives at the root. Type is SF Pro throughout (FRFont uses .system deliberately, to avoid bundling a font); the Foodrun design system wants Cabinet Grotesk on headings and key numbers. Section headers render at 17px uppercase — visually heavier than the rows they label. The seven subject colors are saturated web colors, while the design system's activity palette is desaturated pastel. Cards mix two grounds: neumorphic #F4F5F0 cards (FRCard) and flat white rows (FRRow) on the same page.
